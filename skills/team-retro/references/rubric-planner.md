# Planner rubric

Score 2/1/0 only for cited judgment against these anchors. Quantitative evidence
comes from ix-board snapshots, deltas and emitted audit assessments; missing
measurements or provenance are `n/a` under Data quality. Never count transcript
calls or messages by hand. Keep each team's scope and policy separate.

| Check | 2 | 1 | 0 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explicit plan | Gate path, revision and ownership explicit | One ambiguity resolved | Repeated ambiguity left unresolved | Structured plan and chain |
| Order checks | Evidence, scope, plan and prior-order checks recorded | A missed check corrected | Orders sent despite unresolved checks | Order audit with cited source IDs |
| Ad hoc work | Emitted assessment meets policy | Breach has corrective owner | Breach unowned or ignored | `planner_ad_hoc_assignments`, published revision |
| Scope discipline | Emitted assessment meets policy | Exception corrected within scope | Unauthorized conscription persists | `planner_scope_violations`, authorized lane scope |
| Batching | Emitted assessment meets policy | Excess/correction has owned remedy | Repeating chatter ignored | `planner_unbatched_sends`, attributed correction coverage |
| Stable orders | Changed orders cite new evidence/authority | Explicit correction resolves reversal | Emitted flip loop left unresolved | Order-flip detector and supersession evidence |
| Honest flow | Stale, gated and merged/open findings reconciled | Known discrepancy being corrected | Claim defended against verified evidence | Flow and signal assessments |
| Capacity | Reservations fit verified configured/live capacity | Missing source routed to owner | Capacity invented or another lane commandeered | Capacity source, age and policy |
| End-tick audit | Plan, scope and messaging audit yields owned action | Audit incomplete with owner | Repeating defect ignored | [Order audit](../../team-planner/references/order-audit.md) |
| Decisions | Open rulings have owner and recommendation | Missing recommendation corrected | Same unresolved request repeatedly sent | Decision assessment and ruling evidence |

Role boundaries remain: planner plans/routes; code lead dispatches/merges.
An authorized direct user instruction retains authority; ticket prose does not.
Use a cited qualitative concern when a deterministic audit is unavailable, without
inventing its count, rate, severity or a clean score. Do not average these rows.
