# US-001: Identify planning defects and stalled department flow

## Story

**As a** development department owner
**I want** to see where planned work stops advancing and who can unblock it
**So that** I can correct planning, rework and resource waste across teams.

## Context

A busy pane, a started issue and a queued message can each conceal inactivity.
Operators need comparable history and specific actions, including when the
underlying evidence is unavailable. The same questions recur across projects.
